What travelled and what did not

The link carries a child's first name and date of birth, the state and county the birth was registered in, and a dozen household answers such as how the family is covered. That is all the app needs to rebuild the plan, and it is readable by anyone the link reaches.

It does not carry anything anyone has done: no completed tasks, no confirmation numbers, no photographs of documents. Those never leave the phone they were entered on.

Baby Docs has no household-data account and no household-data server. This page holds nothing and stores nothing: everything after the # in the address stays in your browser and is passed straight to the app. Purchases, if any, are handled separately by Apple and RevenueCat.
