## Does Baby Docs ask for a Social Security number?

No. Baby Docs tracks whether the number has been requested and whether the
card has arrived. It has no field for the number itself, and neither
the one-page summary nor the employer packet ever prints one. There is
no household-data account and no household-data server: everything you
enter, and every document you photograph, stays on your iPhone unless
you choose to send a plan. The plan link carries the answers needed to
rebuild that plan. Photographs are held unreadable while the phone is
locked and are excluded from every backup. Purchases are handled by
Apple and RevenueCat, and those services receive no household data. No
advertising SDK and no behavioural advertising.

## More info

How it is built and why it works that way.

### The Pitch

Nobody tells you what applies to you, and the two items with
real deadlines look exactly like the eighteen that do not.

Generic checklists make this worse. They list a passport next to a
health plan enrollment window as though the two are comparable,
and they cannot tell you which vital records office issues your
child's birth certificate, because they do not know where you are.

Baby Docs asks a short set of questions, and every answer changes
the list. Married or not. Job-based plan or Marketplace. Born in
one state, living in another. Then it gives you the tasks that
apply, ordered by when they close, each one with the documents you
will be asked for and a link to the office that handles it.

It does not file anything. Filing a parentage form, an IRS
election or an insurance enrollment on someone's behalf is a
promise no app should make. It takes you to the official page and
gets out of the way.

### Baby Docs Plus

Every task, every deadline, every document checklist and every

the reminders for the two deadlines that legally close. Nothing

Plus is the timing and the order: reminders for the dates the app

are waiting on, the packet your employer asks for and the

people who want to keep the document vault for good.

### What Mattered

**Only two deadlines are hard.** The 30-day job-based
enrollment window and the 60-day Marketplace one are the only
things the app will send a notification about. Everything else is
a suggestion and is drawn as one. An app that treats a passport
like an insurance window teaches you to ignore both.

**Every sourced rule cites its source.** Each sourced
task carries the government URL its rule came from and the date
someone last read that page, on the task itself rather than behind
an info button. Rules without an honest citation explain the
limit.

**A verified state beats fifty guessed ones.** Where
we have read a state's own vital records page, the task links
straight there and says so. Everywhere else it links to the
federal directory, which has a state picker and is maintained by
someone else.

**Changing an answer never destroys your work.** Move
from a job-based plan to the Marketplace and the plan rebuilds,
but a task you have already completed, assigned or gathered
documents for stays exactly as you left it.

## Frequently asked questions

## What is on a newborn paperwork checklist?

In the United States it usually covers the birth certificate, the Social Security card, adding the baby to your health plan, parental leave and a few optional items like a passport. Baby Docs asks a few questions about your household and shows only the tasks that apply to you.

## How long do I have to add a newborn to health insurance?

A job-based plan must allow at least 30 days after a birth, and the Marketplace is generally 60 days. Baby Docs checks which kind of coverage you have and shows the window that applies, and these are the two deadlines it sends reminders for.

## How do I get a birth certificate for a newborn?

Birth certificates are issued by a state or county vital records office, each with its own forms and fees. Baby Docs links to the office that issues yours and lists the documents to bring.

## How do I know if my baby's Social Security card was requested?

Hospitals usually request the number on the birth registration form, but the request is easy to miss. Baby Docs tracks whether the number has been requested and whether the card has arrived. It never asks for the number itself.

## What documents do I need after my baby is born?

It depends on the task, your state and your coverage. Every task in Baby Docs carries its own list of documents to bring, so you know about things like a certified copy before you are at the counter.

## Does Baby Docs need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed, and the plan works with no signal. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, and there is no household-data server.

## Is Baby Docs free?

Yes. Every task, deadline, document checklist and official link is free, for every child. The optional Plus upgrade adds timing, a document vault and exports, and nothing with a date on it is behind a paywall. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.
