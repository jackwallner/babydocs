Baby Docs Terms of Use

Last updated: August 9, 2026

What the app is

Baby Docs helps a family keep track of the administrative paperwork that follows a birth in the United States. It generates a personalized list of tasks from the answers you give, shows the deadline attached to each one, lists the documents you will be asked for, and links you to the official government page that handles it.

What the app is not

Baby Docs does not give legal, tax, medical, financial or insurance advice. It is an organizational tool. Nothing in it is a substitute for a lawyer, an accountant, a benefits administrator or your own state's rules.

Baby Docs does not file anything for you. It never submits, transmits or signs a government, tax, insurance or legal form on your behalf, and it never logs in to any account of yours. Every task ends with you going to the official site and doing it yourself.

The deadlines in the app are guidance, not guarantees. They come from federal rules that apply generally. Your own health plan, your own employer and your own state can and do set different ones, and those override anything the app shows. Each task links to the source its rule came from, with the date that source was last read. Check it before you rely on a date, especially where money or coverage depends on it.

Rules change. We update the catalog and record when it was last reviewed (visible in Settings), but there will be periods where a rule in the app is behind the rule in the world.

No accounts

There is no account, no login and no server. Everything the app holds is on your iPhone. You can send another parent a link that lets their own phone rebuild the same plan from your answers; from that point the two plans are independent, and neither one updates the other.

A link you send is readable by anyone you send it to, and by anyone they forward it to. It carries your household answers — a birth date, a state, how you are covered — so treat it as you would any message containing those.

Baby Docs Plus

Every task, deadline, document checklist and official link is free for one child, as is sending the plan to another parent. Baby Docs Plus adds the document vault beyond the first twelve weeks, tracking for things you have sent and are waiting on, the employer packet, the printable one-page summary, and further children. It is available as a subscription or as a one-time purchase.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and are managed in your Apple Account settings. A free trial period, where offered, is forfeited if you buy the subscription during the trial. Purchases are handled by Apple; refunds are requested from Apple, not from us.

Documents you have already added to the vault stay readable if a subscription lapses. Lapsing stops you adding new ones; it never removes what is there.

Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate answers. A wrong date of birth or a wrong coverage type produces a wrong plan, and the app has no way to know.
  • Confirm anything that matters against the official source before acting.
  • Do not enter Social Security numbers. The app never asks for one.
  • Use it for your own family, and only send a plan link to people entitled to see it.

Liability

Baby Docs is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for any loss arising from a missed deadline, an out-of-date rule, an incorrect link, or reliance on anything the app displayed. You remain responsible for your own filings.

Ending it

Delete the app and everything goes with it. There is no account to close and no copy of anything on our side, because we never had one.
