Baby Docs Terms of Use

Last updated: August 9, 2026

What the app is

Baby Docs helps a family keep track of the administrative paperwork that follows a birth in the United States. It generates a personalized list of tasks from the answers you give, shows the deadline attached to each one, lists the documents you will be asked for, and links you to the official government page that handles it.

What the app is not

Baby Docs does not give legal, tax, medical, financial or insurance advice. It is an organizational tool. Nothing in it is a substitute for a lawyer, an accountant, a benefits administrator or your own state's rules.

Baby Docs does not file anything for you. It never submits, transmits or signs a government, tax, insurance or legal form on your behalf, and it never logs in to any account of yours. Every task ends with you going to the official site and doing it yourself.

The deadlines in the app are guidance, not guarantees. They come from federal rules that apply generally. Your own health plan, your own employer and your own state can and do set different ones, and those override anything the app shows. Each task links to the source its rule came from, with the date that source was last read. Check it before you rely on a date, especially where money or coverage depends on it.

Rules change. We update the catalog and record when it was last reviewed (visible in Settings), but there will be periods where a rule in the app is behind the rule in the world.

Accounts and families

The app works with no account. If you create one, it belongs to one family, which may contain more than one child. Members with the organizer or parent role can see and change everything in that family. A helper can see it and cannot change it.

Only invite people you intend to give that access to. An invitation works once and expires after 48 hours.

Baby Docs Plus

Every task, deadline, document checklist and official link is free for one child on one device. Baby Docs Plus adds sharing with the other parent and further children, and is available either as a one-time purchase or as a subscription.

One purchase covers the whole family it is bought in. Anyone you invite is never charged as a separate seat.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and are managed in your Apple ID settings. A free trial period, where offered, is forfeited if you buy the subscription during the trial. Purchases are handled by Apple; refunds are requested from Apple, not from us.

Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate answers. A wrong date of birth or a wrong coverage type produces a wrong plan, and the app has no way to know.
  • Confirm anything that matters against the official source before acting.
  • Do not enter Social Security numbers. The app never asks for one.
  • Use it for your own family, and only invite people entitled to see the records.

Ending it

You can delete your account from Settings at any time, or simply delete the app. We may suspend an account that is used to abuse the service or other people.
