Baby Docs Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Overview

Baby Docs is an iPhone app for keeping track of the paperwork that follows a birth: the Social Security card, the birth certificate, health plan enrollment, parental leave and so on. It tells you what applies to your household, when each window closes, and which official office issues each thing.

Everything works on your iPhone with no account. An account exists for one reason, which is sharing the same plan with the other parent.

The one thing we do not collect

Baby Docs never asks for, stores or transmits a Social Security number, for your child or for you. The app tracks whether the number has been requested and whether the card has arrived. It has no field for the number itself, and the one-page summary you can share never prints one. If you type a number into a free-text note, it is stored and synced as ordinary text like any other note, so please do not.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Your child's first name, date of birth, the state and county the birth was registered in, and whether the Social Security card and birth certificate have arrived.
  • Your household answers: the state you live in, how the family is covered, whether the parents are married, and which optional things (passport, 529, parental leave) you want on the list.
  • The generated plan: each task, its deadline, which documents you have gathered, who it is assigned to, and any confirmation or tracking numbers you choose to record.
  • Any notes you write.

The local database is encrypted at rest by iOS until the first unlock after the phone boots.

What leaves your iPhone, and when

Nothing, unless you sign in and set up a shared family. Until then there is no account, no server copy, and no network request carrying anything you have entered.

If you do share, then from that point:

  • The plan above is copied to our database (hosted by Supabase, in the United States) so the other parent's iPhone can read it.
  • Your display name, taken from Sign in with Apple. If you use Apple's private relay address, we never see your real one.
  • A device token, if you turn on deadline reminders, so a reminder can be delivered.
  • Your purchase status, handled by RevenueCat. We never see a card number or any payment detail; Apple handles the transaction.

Who can see it

  • The people in your family, and nobody else. Access is enforced by row-level security in the database, not only by the app.
  • Organizer and parent can see and change everything in their own family.
  • Helper can see the plan and what is outstanding, and cannot change it.
  • Nobody outside your family can see anything in it.

A full copy stays on each iPhone, so the plan opens with no network. That is deliberate: the moment you most need to know which three documents the clerk is about to ask for is while you are standing at the counter.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not file, submit or transmit any government, tax, insurance or legal form on your behalf. The app links you to the official page and you file it yourself.
  • We do not read, hold or connect to your health records, your insurance account, your bank or any government account.

Children

Baby Docs is for parents, and is not directed at children. The information it holds about a child is the small set listed above, entered by that child's own parent.

Deleting things

  • Delete a child, a task or a note in the app and it is removed from your family's copy everywhere.
  • Settings → Delete account removes your account and your membership. If you are the organizer and someone else is in the family, the family passes to them; if you are the last member, the family and its plan are deleted.
  • Deleting the app removes the copy on that iPhone. If you shared, delete your account first, or the shared copy stays.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export (the one-page summary shares as plain text) and delete everything the app holds, from inside the app, without asking us. If you would rather we did it, email the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who can see it, the app will say so before the change takes effect.
