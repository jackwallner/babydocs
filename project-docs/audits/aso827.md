## Executive directive

Keep the `Baby Docs` brand, the current App Store title, and the current subtitle. The title has become Apple's only autocomplete suggestion for `newborn paperwork`, while the app has no usable post-launch acquisition baseline and zero reported sales units in the two available launch weeks. A rename would discard the one positive search signal before the app has enough data to judge it.

Ship the current US keyword field unchanged until a real baseline exists. `documents` is broad and difficult, but the strongest available alternative, `checklist`, resolves mainly to hospital-bag, shopping, and pregnancy products. Test that one-token swap only after the current field has at least two complete reporting periods.

Keep the current first-three screenshot order. It already tells the correct search-grid story: what is due, why it applies, and what to bring. Repair the rest of the package. The live sixth screenshot is a pricing screen presented as document-vault proof, and the pending 1.1.0 draft removes it without adding a replacement, leaving only five frames. Build a six-frame set with literal sharing and on-device document proof, then recapture it from the exact submission commit.

## Product truth and claim boundaries

### Supported positioning

- Seven household questions produce a household-specific US task plan.
- Rules cover all 50 states and DC at two disclosed verification depths.
- The plan orders work and identifies the next hard deadline.
- Job-based health plans must provide at least a 30-day newborn enrollment window, and Marketplace enrollment is generally 60 days, with linked sources and disclaimers.
- Every task explains why it applies, what to bring, where to go, and when the source was reviewed.
- Hard-deadline reminders are free.
- Parents can share the plan seed through a link. The two plans do not stay synchronized.
- Household data stays on device. Purchases are handled by Apple and RevenueCat.
- The app tracks Social Security number status, never the number itself.
- Vault images are file-protected, excluded from backup, and not exposed through sharing or export APIs.
- The app never files, signs, or submits anything.

### Claims that must stay out of metadata and screenshots

- `Files your paperwork`
- `Guarantees you never miss a deadline`
- `Legal advice`, `tax advice`, `medical advice`, or `insurance advice`
- `Covers every US rule` or equivalent absolute breadth claims
- `Every deadline is official`
- `Live government updates`
- `Automatic partner sync`
- `Cloud backup` or `sync across devices`
- `Nothing is uploaded`, because RevenueCat receives purchase-related data. Narrow claims about household answers and vault photos are supportable.
- Any statement that Baby Docs stores a Social Security number
- Any fixed government fee or processing-time promise
- Any non-US government coverage
