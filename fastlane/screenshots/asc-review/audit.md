## Market brief

- Category: newborn administrative paperwork planners
- Audience: US families in the first weeks after a birth who need to know what applies, when it closes, and what to bring
- Problem: Generic checklists omit state, household, insurance, and parentage details, turning one urgent deadline into a pile of tabs and handouts.
- Advantage: Baby Docs builds from the family's answers, then shows the deadlines, official sources, and document checklists that fit that household on one device.
- Competitive context: The app competes with generic newborn checklists, hospital handouts, and calendar reminders. It wins on personal applicability and source-backed tasks, while staying on-device and never filing anything for the family.
