The paperwork after a baby is not hard. The problem is that nobody tells you what applies to you, and the two items with real deadlines look exactly like the eighteen that do not.

Baby Docs asks six screens of questions, and every answer changes the list. Married or not. A job-based health plan or the Marketplace. Born in one state, living in another. Then it gives you the tasks that actually apply to your family, ordered by when they close.

WHAT YOU GET

- A personalized plan, not a generic checklist. Every task says in one sentence why it applies to your household.
- The real deadlines, marked as such. A job-based plan must allow at least 30 days after a birth to add the baby. The Marketplace is generally 60. Most of the rest of the list has no deadline at all, and the app says so instead of pretending otherwise.
- What to bring. Every task carries its own document list, so you find out about the certified copy before you are standing at the counter, not after.
- Where to actually go. Birth certificates are issued by state and county offices with their own forms and fees. You get sent to the right one.
- Somewhere to put the confirmation number. Whatever came back lives on the task it belongs to.
- Reminders that fire only for deadlines that genuinely close, so you do not end up muting the app.

IT SHOWS ITS WORKING

Every rule links to the government page it came from, with the date that page was last read, right there on the task. Settings tells you when the whole rule set was last reviewed. A paperwork app asking you to trust it about dates that cost money should have to show you where the date came from.

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

It never files anything for you. No government, tax, insurance or legal form is submitted, signed or transmitted on your behalf, and it never logs in to any account of yours. It takes you to the official page and gets out of the way.

It never asks for a Social Security number. It tracks whether the number has been requested and whether the card has arrived, and there is no field for the number itself. The shareable one-page summary never prints one.

It is not a baby tracker. No feeds, no weights, no growth charts.

WORKS WITH NO SIGNAL

Everything renders from a copy on your phone. No account is needed to start.

BABY DOCS PLUS

Every task, deadline, document checklist and official link is free, for one child, on one device. Nothing with a date on it is ever behind a paywall.

Plus adds any further children, so a second baby inherits your household answers instead of asking again, and the printable one-page summary you can hand to someone else. Available as a one-time purchase or as a subscription.

Baby Docs gives no legal, tax, medical or insurance advice. Deadlines set by your own plan, employer or state override anything shown in the app. Check the linked source before you rely on a date.
